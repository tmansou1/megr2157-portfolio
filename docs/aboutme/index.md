#About Me

My name is Taye Mansour, and I'm a second year mechanical engineering student at UNC Charlotte. 
I always had a love for numbers and math growing up through middle and highschool, this love of math and the luck of having some great teachers pushed me to try engineering. Also growing up messing with legos and taking apart things to understand how they worked, and watching fascinating videos on youtube has had a great effect on me liking the thought of engineering. What pulled me toward mechanical specifically, over other engineering disciplines, is more basic: I like being able to see how something works. In mechanical systems, motion, force, and geometry are usually visible and physically intuitive. That's less true in fields like electrical engineering, where the underlying behavior is often hidden inside a circuit rather than something you can observe directly. I understand concepts better when I can see and physically trace the cause and effect, and mechanical engineering plays to that, but I am still open to learning aspects of each discipline.

I don't yet know which corner of the field I want to specialize in: design, manufacturing, robotics, or something I haven't been exposed to yet. At this point, I'm still learning about engineering as a whole and haven't yet stuck with anything. I hope this MEGR 2157 design class and future classes will help me find what I will be passionate for.

What I do know is the kind of engineer I want to become, one who can explain the reasoning behind a decision as clearly as the decision itself, and who treats a design choice as defensible because it's been tested and reasoned through, not just because it happens to work.
